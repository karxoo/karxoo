## 🚀 Quest Log: Karxoo's Journey to Full-Stack Mastery

#### 🧙‍♂️ Frontend Warlock | 🛠️ Systems Architect in Training | 🐈 Chief Cat Servant

</div>

I’m a **Software Developer** who specializes in crafting sleek, bug-free (mostly) frontends using **Angular** and **Blazor**. Currently, I'm on a daring expedition into the **Backend** wilderness. My mission? To finally understand what the database is actually thinking and achieve the legendary **Full-Stack** status.

---

### 🛠️ What I'm working on:
* 🎨 **The Visuals:** Refactoring **Angular** and **Blazor** components until they are "Chef's Kiss" perfection.
* 🏗️ **The Foundation:** Leveling up in **.NET** and **Spring Boot** so I can finally stop blaming the Backend for everything.
* 📱 **On the Go:** Crafting mobile experiences with **Flutter** and **Kotlin** because the world fits in a pocket.
* 🎯 **The End Goal:** Achieving the legendary **Full-Stack Cape** 🎓

### 🌱 Currently Learning:
* 📊 **Database Mastery:** Diving deep into **MS SQL** optimization—because a fast frontend deserves a lightning-fast database.
* 🤖 **Automation:** Enhancing CI/CD workflows with **GitHub Actions** to make the robots do the hard work.
